Problem: List Reversal

Write a Prolog predicate `reverse_list(L, R)` that reverses a list.
The predicate should be true when R is the reverse of list L.

For example:
- reverse_list([1,2,3], [3,2,1]) should succeed
- reverse_list([], []) should succeed  
- reverse_list([a], [a]) should succeed

This is a classic recursive problem. Think about:
1. What is the base case? (empty list)
2. How to reverse the tail and append the head at the end?
